1. What Happens If I Miss My Health Policy Renewal Date
A grace period of 30 days applies after the due date during which continuity of cover is preserved but no new claims arising in the gap window are payable for illnesses contracted during the break. Beyond 30 days the policy lapses and fresh underwriting with fresh waiting periods applies.

2. How Does No Claim Bonus Work When I Switch Motor Insurers
Your accrued No Claim Bonus belongs to you as the insured person, not to the vehicle sale alone. Present the renewal notice or NCB certificate from your previous insurer to receive the equivalent NCB slab with the new insurer at porting.

3. Can I Add A Newborn Baby To My Family Health Policy
Yes. The newborn is covered from day one under SecurePlus v2.1 until the next renewal without additional premium; at renewal add the child to the schedule with name and date of birth to continue cover.

4. How Do I Transfer Vehicle Insurance To The Buyer Of My Car
Hand over the policy and apply for a ownership transfer endorsement within 14 days of sale using Form 29 and Form 30 copies. Your No Claim Bonus stays with you and can be used on your next vehicle within 90 days.

5. What Documents Are Needed For A Cashless Hospital Admission
Provide the health policy number or e-card, photo identity proof and the hospital admission note. For planned admissions request pre-authorisation at least 48 hours before admission through the network hospital insurance desk.

6. Is My Premium Refunded If I Cancel During The Free Look Period
Health policies carry a 15-day free look period from receipt of the document. Cancellation within free look refunds the premium less proportionate risk cover for the days on risk, medical check-up costs and stamp duty where applicable.

7. How Long Does A Reimbursement Claim Take To Settle
Reimbursement claims are settled within 30 days of receiving complete documentation per clause 7.1 of SecurePlus v2.1. Incomplete documents pause the clock until the missing items are provided.
