1. Purpose Of This Endorsement
This endorsement revises the vehicle age eligibility and claim limits for the Zero Depreciation add-on under DriveCare Motor Package Policy v2.4, aligning with revised tariff filings.

2. Revised Eligibility
The Zero Depreciation add-on is available for private cars up to 5 years of vehicle age at first purchase of the add-on. Continuation is permitted up to 7 years for renewals without break; beyond 7 years the add-on lapses and standard depreciation applies.

3. Revised Claim Limit
A maximum of two zero depreciation claims are admissible per policy year combined across all vehicles on the schedule. From the third claim onward, parts depreciation applies per Annexure M of the base policy.

4. Premium Loading For High Risk Localities
Vehicles garaged in postal codes listed in the high-theft zone annexure attract a 10 percent loading on the zero depreciation premium component. The loading is disclosed in the quotation before payment.
