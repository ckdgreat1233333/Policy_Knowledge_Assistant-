1. Scope Of Cover
This indemnity policy covers inpatient hospitalisation, day-care procedures listed in Annexure A, pre and post hospitalisation expenses for 60 and 90 days respectively, and emergency ambulance charges up to Rs 2,000 per hospitalisation for the insured persons named in the schedule.

1.1 Sum Insured Options
Sum insured options are Rs 5 lakh, Rs 10 lakh, Rs 25 lakh, Rs 50 lakh and Rs 1 crore per family per policy year on a family floater basis.

1.2 Restoration Benefit
On complete or partial utilisation of the sum insured in a policy year, the sum insured is restored once without additional premium for subsequent claims in the same year. Restoration does not apply to claims for the same illness for which the earlier claim was made.

2. Eligibility And Entry Age
Adults aged 18 to 65 years may enrol as proposers; dependent children are covered from 91 days to 25 years if financially independent education is continuing. Renewal is lifelong provided continuity of cover is maintained without a break exceeding 30 days.

3. Waiting Periods
A continuous waiting period of 30 days applies from the commencement date for all illnesses except accidents. Specified procedures listed in Annexure B carry a 24-month waiting period.

3.1 Pre-Existing Diseases
Pre-existing diseases are covered after a continuous waiting period of 36 months of first enrollment, provided premiums are paid when due and no material misstatement was made in the proposal form.

3.2 Maternity And Newborn Cover
Maternity expenses are covered up to Rs 75,000 for normal delivery and Rs 100,000 for caesarean section, subject to a 24-month continuous waiting period. The newborn baby is covered from day one under the family floater without additional premium for the remainder of the policy year.

4. Exclusions
This policy does not cover expenses arising from or attributable to non-medical items as per Annexure C, cosmetic or aesthetic treatments unless medically necessary following accident or cancer, dental treatment unless requiring hospitalisation, experimental or unproven treatments, treatment outside the geographical limits of India unless the overseas treatment rider is purchased, self-inflicted injury or attempted suicide, participation in hazardous sports, war and nuclear perils, and treatment for alcohol or substance abuse.

4.1 Specific Disease Exclusions
No cover is admissible for internal congenital conditions existing before entry, HIV and AIDS related treatments, and infertility treatments including IVF and surcharge procedures.

5. Co-Payment And Room Rent Limits
A co-payment of 10 percent applies to all claims for insured persons aged 61 years and above at the time of admission. Room rent is payable up to single private room category; associated procedure costs are proportionately reduced if a higher category room is chosen.

6. Cashless Network
Cashless facility is available only at network hospitals listed in the current empanelment directory published quarterly by the insurer. For planned admissions, intimation must be given at least 48 hours before hospitalisation; for emergencies, within 24 hours of admission.

7. Claims Procedure
Claims must be intimated through the claims helpline or portal with policy number, patient details and hospital information. Documents required include discharge summary, original bills, prescriptions, diagnostic reports and KYC of the proposer.

7.1 Claim Decision Timeline
Cashless claims receive a decision within 2 hours of the final authorisation request for emergencies and within 6 working hours for planned admissions. Reimbursement claims are settled within 30 days of receipt of complete documentation.

8. Premium Loading And Revision
Premium rates are guaranteed for the first policy year only. At renewal the insurer may revise premiums across age bands with prior approval of the Authority and 90 days notice to the insured. A no-claim bonus of 10 percent of sum insured per claim-free year accrues up to a maximum of 50 percent.

9. Portability And Continuity
Portability requests under applicable regulations are accepted at least 45 days before renewal, with credit for accrued waiting periods when the portability declaration is verified.

10. Grievance Redressal
Grievances are first addressed by the servicing branch within 15 days. Unresolved grievances may be escalated to the Insurance Ombudsman under the applicable redressal scheme. This clause is procedural and does not extend coverage.
