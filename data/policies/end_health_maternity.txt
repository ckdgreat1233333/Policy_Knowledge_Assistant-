Supersedes: clause 3.2 of pol_health_secureplus v2.0
Approved By: Product Committee

1. Purpose Of This Endorsement
This endorsement revises clause 3.2 of SecurePlus Family Health Policy v2.0 following IRDAI circular IRDAI/HLT/MISC/CIR/089/2026 on standardisation of maternity benefits. All other clauses of v2.0 remain unchanged.

2. Revised Maternity Cover
Maternity expenses are covered up to Rs 75,000 for normal delivery and Rs 100,000 for caesarean section, subject to a reduced continuous waiting period of 24 months from first enrollment. The earlier waiting period of 36 months stands superseded for policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.

2.1 Newborn Cover From Day One
The newborn baby is covered from birth under the family floater without additional premium until the next renewal, when the child must be added to the schedule.

2.2 Pre And Post Natal Expenses
Pre-natal expenses up to Rs 10,000 and post-natal expenses up to Rs 15,000 incurred within 90 days of delivery are admissible within the maternity limits stated above.

3. Applicability And Transition
For in-force policies renewed on or after the effective date, the reduced waiting period applies prospectively from renewal date and completed waiting time under v2.0 counts toward the 24-month requirement. No retrospective reopening of settled claims arises from this revision.
